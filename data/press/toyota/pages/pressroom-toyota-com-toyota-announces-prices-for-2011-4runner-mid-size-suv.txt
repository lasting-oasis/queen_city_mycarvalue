Toyota Announces Prices for 2011 4Runner Mid-Size SUV

July 30, 2010

Read More

Share on Facebook

Share on Linkedin

Send email

Copy Link

TORRANCE, Calif., July 30, 2010 – – Toyota Motor Sales (TMS), USA, Inc., announced manufacturer?s suggested retail prices (MSRP) today for the 2011 4Runner mid-sized sport utility vehicle (SUV).  The 2011 4Runner will begin reaching Toyota dealerships in September.

The base MSRP for the 2011 4Runner will range from $29,525 for the SR5 4×2 V6 with a five-speed automatic transmission to $39,685 for the Limited grade 4×4 V6 with a five-speed automatic.  The base MSRP?s reflect changes that range from a decrease of $115, or 0.3 percent on some models to an increase of $350, or 1.2 percent on others.

All-new in 2010, the 4Runner carries over virtually unchanged for 2011 model year.

Base MSRPs for the 2011 4Runner do not include a delivery, processing, and handling (DPH) fee of $810.  The DPH fee for vehicles distributed by Southeast Toyota (SET) and Gulf States Toyota (GST) may vary.
