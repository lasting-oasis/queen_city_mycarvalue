Toyota Announces Pricing for Refreshed 2015 Prius v

November 19, 2014

Read More

Send email

New Styling and Product Enhancements Inside and Out

Entune Audio & Advanced Technology Updates

Improved Grade Strategy

TORRANCE, Calif., November 19, 2014 – The 2015 Toyota Prius v – “the big Prius” – arrives for the holidays with exciting styling and advanced technology enhancements that add sophistication, convenience and a more active feel inside and out.  Toyota today announced manufacturers’ suggested retail prices (MSRP) for the refreshed 2015 Prius v .

The base MSRP for the 2015 Prius v will range from $26,675 for the Prius v Two (reflecting a price decrease over the outgoing model) and will top out at $30,935 for the premium Prius v Five.  The 2015 Prius v will begin arriving at dealerships in December.

v  For Versatility
The 2015 Prius v continues to offer more cargo space than most small SUV’s with the highest combined MPG of an SUV, crossover utility vehicle or wagon.  Each grade comes with an array of standard features to meet the needs of a wide range of buyers.
Standard features on the Prius v Two includes:

Redesigned new front fascia with a black accent lower grille, and distinctive LED tail lamps

Entune Audio with Entune Multimedia Bundle

Integrated backup camera display

16-inch alloy wheels with full wheel covers with a new premium finish

SofTex ® center console with armrest and storage compartment

6-way adjustable driver’s seat with; 4-way adjustable fold flat front passenger seat

60/40 split sliding reclining fold-down rear bench seat with adjustable headrests

Prius v Three adds:

Entune Premium Audio with Navigation and App Suite (includes the Entune Multimedia Bundle with SiriusXM All Access Radio with a 3-month complimentary trial and HD radio)

New full-color 4.2-inch TFT Multi-Information Display

Driver power lumbar support, passenger seatback pocket and rear seat armrest

LED accessory lights and auto-on/off headlights

3-Door Smart Key System

Newly designed lower rear bumper treatment

2015 Prius v Pricing 2/2/2/2

An all-new Prius v Four model is now available and adds:

SofTex ® -trimmed heated front seats; 8-way power-adjustable driver’s seat with power lumbar support; 4-way adjustable fold-flat front passenger seat

Auto-dimming rearview mirror with HomeLink ®

Prius v Five adds:

A Toyota-first : Single LED projector low and high-beam headlights with LED accent lights and with auto-on/off feature

17-inch alloy wheels with a new premium finish

Integrated fog lights

An updated and high value-added Advanced Technology Package is available on the Prius v Five and features:

Entune ® Premium JBL Audio with Navigation and App Suite – includes the Entune Multimedia Bundle

Dynamic Radar Cruise Control (DRCC)

Pre-Collision System (PCS)

Lane Departure Alert (LDA)

Auto High Beam (AHB)

Panoramic View Moonroof with power sliding sunshades

Safety Connect

The 2015 Prius v also adds three new exterior colors including Absolutely Red, Attitude Black Metallic and Toasted Walnut Pearl.  The new colors complement a new fabric design that comes in Black or Ash while SofTex ® is offered in Bisque, Black or Ash.

The MSRP for the 2015 Prius v does not include the delivery, processing, and handling (DPH) fee of $825. The DPH fee for vehicles distributed by Southeast Toyota (SET) and Gulf States Toyota (GST) may vary.
