Toyota Announces Prices For 2010 Sienna

May 08, 2009

Read More

Share on Facebook

Share on Linkedin

Send email

Copy Link

TORRANCE, Calif., May 8, 2009 – Toyota Motor Sales (TMS), U.S.A., Inc., today announced manufacturer?s suggested retail prices (MSRP) for the 2010 Sienna van will remain unchanged from 2009 model year prices. Sienna carries over unchanged for 2010 and will arrive at dealerships later this month.

The Sienna is available in four model grades: CE, LE, XLE and the top-of-the-line Limited. All four models come in front-wheel drive and the LE, XLE, and Limited are also available in all-wheel-drive. The CE and LE are available in both seven- and eight-passenger configurations; others are seven-passenger models.

The base MSRPs for the Sienna range from $24,540 for the seven-passenger CE FWD model to $37,865 for the top-of-the-line Limited AWD model.

Base MSRPs do not include a delivery, processing, and handling (DPH) fee of $800. The DPH fee for vehicles distributed by Southeast Toyota (SET) and Gulf States Toyota (GST) may vary.
